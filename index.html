About IDEA · Case File

Who We Are

Innovations for Democratic Engagement and Action is a non‑secular, non‑aligned democracy ideas incubation hub and policy think tank, dedicated to shaping Uganda's democratic landscape.

Established primarily to support young people in re‑channeling their energy, utilizing their demographic potential to pursue a progressive democratic future, IDEA serves as a platform for transformative ideas aimed at enhancing good governance, citizen participation, and accountability.

Vision
Vision

Our vision is a Ugandan body politic that is led by progressive young leaders, thinkers and innovators who profess a shared value system and are interested in a common development agenda.

Mission
Mission

Why We Exist

The gap we close

12M Young Ugandans

— restless with ideas and aspirations for progress.

Uganda’s population boom has gifted the country with twelve million young adults, many of whose minds are bubbling and restless with ideas and aspirations for socioeconomic progress and political revolution.

The Gap

The prevailing political order and architecture of the economy are both too weak, malformed, and externalized to constructively channel that energy and those dreams into building blocks for a more secure and predictable future. IDEA aspires to provide space and support for the fine‑tuning, incubation, and actualization of pro‑democracy and development concepts.

Field Note

At the core of IDEA’s work is the development of civic engagement innovations based on digital technology and other non‑conventional mechanisms to address Uganda’s salient policy and citizenship challenges. IDEA spearheads big data initiatives, positioning itself as a comprehensive repository for datasets related to governance trends and the development trajectory. Through cutting‑edge research and deep thinking coalescing, IDEA generates knowledge that informs public policy and discourse while providing civic actors with credible evidence and tools for democratic engagement.

Flagship Programme Case 01 · What We Do
Innovations Lab

An interactive and broadminded centre to develop new ideas

IDEA’s innovations lab is anchored on developing, facilitating and promoting governance and social entrepreneurial innovations by development practitioners, democracy activists and young people.

The Lab aims at encouraging actors to come up with ground‑breaking approaches for participation in the governance and development processes, with a specific bias towards the uptake of new technology in participation. Its core objective is to channel young people’s intellect, thoughts and energy into actionable projects for social, economic and democratic impact.

IDEA lab is thus a seedbed for creative thinking, learning and experimentation in a hands‑on, learn‑while‑doing model. Through this lab, young people and other actors are able to test and devise new ideas, products, services and public advocacy campaigns.

The Method

How the Lab Works

Seven steps from a raw idea to a working prototype.

01

Identification of challenges in the status quo, and/or emerging opportunities — encouraging young people and activists to come up with innovations in response.

02

Provision of an anchor that can listen to the ideas in respect to the identified challenge, supporting the processing of ideas and putting them on paper in form of concepts.

03

Search for new technologies which could help, and identification of the leading experts and professionals to provide training and host workshops that add value to the ideas.

04

Organising a collaborative, agile framework for innovation between professionals and activists, where the players take the new tool, idea or technology and explore how it can be used in their field.

05

Advising on possible partners or like‑minded actors who can help take the ideas forward, as well as potential end users of the innovation.

06

Where need be, working with the innovators to execute their ideas directly.

07

Adding a social enterprise aspect to every idea as a way of financing and sustainability, with follow‑up support to maintain and upgrade the technologies in use.

Research & Consultancy Case 04 · What We Do
Research and Consultancy Services

Purposeful and cutting‑edge research to change status‑quo

IDEA conducts specialized, purposeful and cutting edge political and social research on behalf of NGOs, political parties, parastatals and government.

IDEA also conducts in‑house research on emerging contexts and in response to status quo.
